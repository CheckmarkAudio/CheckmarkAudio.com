# Home Page Blueprint

The homepage is the main sales page. It should help a first-time visitor understand what Checkmark Audio does, who it helps, why to trust the studio, what to click next, and how the school fits into the larger ecosystem.

## Page Goal

Convert first-time visitors into one of four actions:

- Book a session.
- View services and pricing.
- Hear or see proof of the work.
- Explore the school pathway.

## Audience Positioning

Primary audience: music artists, singers, and bands.

Secondary audience: anyone who needs professional recording, mixing, or mastering, including voice-over artists, streamers, filmmakers, podcast-style clients, and other creative audio clients.

## Primary KPI

Homepage `Book a Session` clicks.

## Secondary KPIs

- Services/pricing clicks.
- Portfolio play/clicks.
- Tap-to-call or tap-to-text clicks on mobile.
- School pathway clicks.
- Contact/location clicks.

**1. How do I book a session?**
The easiest way is to book a free consultation. You can also call or text 505-267-0558, email CheckmarkAudio@gmail.com, or send the booking form with your service, preferred date/time, and a short project description.

**2. Do you offer free consultations?**
Yes. In a free consultation you meet the engineers, tour the studio, discuss your project, choose the right service path, and get pricing guidance.

**3. How much does recording cost?**
Vocal recording starts at $50/hr. General studio and engineering services start at $65/hr. Band recording starts at $75/hr. Larger or more complex projects — full productions, EPs, albums, artist media packages — get a custom project-based quote after we learn the scope of your project.

**5. Do you mix and master songs?**
Yes. Mixing balances vocals, instruments, effects, tone, space, and dynamics so the song feels polished and emotionally clear. Mastering prepares the final mix for release with final loudness, tone, spacing, and quality control. Editing, vocal tuning, mix prep, and a full release-ready package are also available.

**6. Can you help finish an unfinished song?**
Yes. Song development takes an unfinished song idea and turns it into a more complete production, with arrangement help, vocal production, and creative direction available along the way.

**7. Do you work with rappers and vocalists?**
Yes. Vocal recording sessions capture clean, focused takes for singers, rappers, vocalists, and songwriters — lead vocals, doubles, harmonies, ad-libs, and punch-ins. Rap recording sessions are built for efficient leads, doubles, ad-libs, punch-ins, and creative vocal layering.

**8. Do you record bands?**
Yes. Band recording supports full groups and multi-part sessions — drums, guitars, bass, keys, vocals, and multi-performer projects. Studio A includes an instrument room and space for larger sessions.

**9. Do you record podcasts or voice-over?**
Yes. Podcast recording covers clean spoken-word for podcasts, interviews, educational content, and branded content. Voice-over recording covers narration, commercials, video voice-over, and business content.

**10. What is the difference between Studio A and Studio B?**
Studio A is the larger, premier space — a spacious acoustically treated vocal booth, an equipment-focused control room, and an instrument room — best for premium vocal sessions, full production, bands, acoustic recording, and larger projects. Studio B is a focused room with a high-end vocal booth and an intimate recording environment — best for vocals, demos, writing sessions, rap sessions, and smaller or budget-conscious projects.

**11. Do you offer music production?**
Yes. Production support includes building the sound, arrangement, instrumentation, and direction of a song, plus custom beat creation, arrangement help, vocal production, and song development.

**12. Do you offer artist photography or promotional services?**
Yes, as add-ons for artists who want release support: artist photography, cover art, lyric videos, visualizers, EPK materials, and promotional content.

**13. Do you teach audio engineering?**
Yes. Checkmark Audio School is the studio's education pathway for people who want to learn audio engineering, music production, and studio skills through hands-on education connected to a real working studio.
