# Checkmark Audio Website Master Build Prompt

Build the Checkmark Audio website as a professional, client-facing sales system for a recording studio in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

The site must be based on `CHECKMARK_AUDIO_WEBSITE_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.docx`. Use that DOCX as the highest source of truth. This Markdown file is the plain-language build prompt that gathers the important approved details into one place for Codex, Claude, or any future website builder.

The goal is not just to make a nice website. The goal is to create a complete, clear, trustworthy path for visitors to understand the studio, choose a service, hear and see proof, and book or contact Checkmark Audio.

## Core Positioning

Checkmark Audio is a professional recording studio in Albuquerque offering recording, production, mixing, mastering, and artist development services for artists who want polished, release-ready sound.

Checkmark Audio works with vocalists, rappers, singer-songwriters, bands, podcasters, creators, students, and anyone looking for professional audio support in a comfortable studio environment.

Clients should be able to explore recording, production, mixing, mastering, artist media, and education services, then choose the option that best fits their project.

Checkmark Audio should feel skilled, professional, down-to-earth, and serious about helping artists make release-ready music without making the process feel intimidating.

## Definition Of Done

The website is considered complete when a first-time visitor can clearly understand what Checkmark Audio does, who the studio is for, what services are available, what starting prices are or how to get a quote, why they should trust the studio, how to book or contact the studio, where the studio is located, what the work sounds and looks like, how Checkmark Audio School connects to the studio, and what next step to take without confusion.
