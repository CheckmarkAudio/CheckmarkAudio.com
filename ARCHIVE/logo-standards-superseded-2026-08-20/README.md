# Superseded Checkmark Audio Logo Material

Archived: 2026-08-20

Bridget designated three new 2000×2000 transparent logo exports as the official standards and directed the project to archive the rest of the comparable Checkmark Audio logo treatments.

This folder preserves the retired files; nothing here is active website media. The current official black, gold, and white standards live in `MEDIA/IMAGES/` and are documented in `SEO/LOGO_ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.

- `active-images/`: retired files previously stored directly in the active image library
- `sitewide-page-copies/`: retired page-assignment copies
- `original-sources/`: older source variants formerly stored under `MEDIA/ORIGINALS/`
- `support-files/`: validation or generation files tied to the retired standards
- `previous-canonical-set-before-desktop-cm/`: the black, gold, and white canonical files replaced by Bridget's later `CM - LOGO` exports, including the former sitewide gold copy
- `logotype-studies-2026-08-20/`: the experimental wordmark and lockup studies that preceded the final three-file main-logo decision

Third-party marks, separate Checkmark brands, and photographs containing physical Checkmark Audio signs were intentionally left outside this archive.
