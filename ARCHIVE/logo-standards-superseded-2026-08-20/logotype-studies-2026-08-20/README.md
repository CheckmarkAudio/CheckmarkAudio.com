# Checkmark Audio Logotype Studies

Status: exploratory visual study; no logotype is approved.

## Source sweep

This study reviewed the active August 19 editorial references and mockups, the July 24 website visual concepts, and the active brand guide. The clearest recurring signals were:

- Editorial fashion-house typography rather than a thin geometric tech wordmark.
- High-contrast serif forms with tall proportions and controlled spacing.
- Deep black, warm cream, champagne gold, and only a micro red accent.
- Monumental typography balanced by intimate studio imagery.
- Condensed sans typography as a supporting label voice, not the primary brand voice.
- Premium but human; never nightclub, streetwear, generic luxury, or corporate technology.

## Isotype handling

`isotype-unchanged-crop.png` is a pixel crop from `MEDIA/IMAGES/checkmark-audio-headphones-microphone-shirt-logo.webp`. Its geometry was not redrawn, traced, cleaned, or replaced. It was only cropped and proportionally scaled for consistent lockup comparison.

## Six directions

1. **Editorial Roman** — decisive fashion-editorial authority.
2. **Poetic Editorial** — softer artist-first expression with an italic second line.
3. **Modern Bodoni** — crisp, premium, architectural contrast.
4. **Editorial + Studio** — expressive serif brand name with direct sans production label.
5. **Warm Heritage** — credible, established, and human.
6. **Quiet Modern** — restrained humanist sans for maximum versatility.

## Initial design judgment

The strongest fits for the approved visual direction are **01 Editorial Roman**, **02 Poetic Editorial**, and **04 Editorial + Studio**. Direction 04 is the most flexible system because the serif carries the editorial personality while the tracked sans `AUDIO` connects naturally to navigation, buttons, metadata, and studio labels.

Direction 05 is trustworthy but less distinctive. Direction 06 is usable but moves closest to generic modern branding. Direction 03 is premium, though its sharper fashion character may feel less warm than the studio experience.

## Refined 04 + 06 round

The `refined-04-06/` folder responds to Bridget's preference for directions 04 and 06, with the heavy condensed `AUDIO` from the first 04 removed. All six new candidates use a much lighter `AUDIO` treatment:

1. Cormorant + Optima
2. Didot + Helvetica Neue Thin
3. Bodoni + Avenir Next Ultra Light
4. Cormorant + Avenir Next Condensed Ultra Light
5. Optima + Helvetica Neue Light
6. Optima + Helvetica Neue Thin in title case

The most balanced starting points are refined directions **01, 04, and 05**. Direction 02 is the sharpest luxury/fashion interpretation. Direction 06 is the softest and most approachable.

## Same-family round

The `same-family/` folder tests six lockups in which **Checkmark** and **Audio** use the same typeface. The isotype remains unchanged. Color, scale, case, and spacing create hierarchy without mixing font families.

1. Optima, uppercase and stacked
2. Optima, title case and stacked
3. Optima, title case on one line
4. Baskerville, uppercase and stacked
5. Baskerville, title case and stacked
6. Baskerville, title case on one line

Directions **01 and 03** are the cleanest continuations of the preferred 06 style. Directions **04 and 06** carry more of the editorial character from 04 while keeping the typography completely unified.

## Uniform Optima correction

The `uniform-optima/` folder corrects the same-family round by removing every typographic and color change between the two words. In all six lockups, **CHECKMARK AUDIO** uses:

- Optima Regular
- Uppercase letters
- One warm-cream color
- One consistent weight
- Equal treatment for both words

Only the physical layout, tracking, and line arrangement vary. Direction **01** is the direct corrected version of the preferred earlier 01.

## Experimental minimalist round

The `experimental-minimal/` folder pushes the uniform Optima direction through negative space and unconventional layout. The brand name remains uppercase Optima Regular in one warm-cream color throughout; the isotype artwork remains unchanged.

1. Microline
2. Offset axis
3. Vertical spine
4. Hairline
5. Split horizon
6. Quiet stack

Directions **01 and 04** are the most immediately usable minimalist lockups. Directions **03 and 05** are the most experimental.

## Selected direction 04

Direction **04 / Hairline** was selected. The `selected-04/` folder contains its final white transparent exports. The wordmark and hairline use exact white (`#FFFFFF`) to match `MEDIA/IMAGES/checkmark-audio-logo-official-white-transparent.png`; the official isotype is reused without recoloring or reshaping.
