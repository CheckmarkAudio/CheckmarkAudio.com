FAQ

Quick answers.

How do I book?

Use the form or the calendar below, or call/text 505-267-0558.

What does it cost?

Vocals from $50/hr · studio & engineering from $65/hr · bands from $75/hr. Rates are the same in Studio A and Studio B. Bigger projects get a custom quote.

What should I bring to a session?

Anything relevant to your project — reference tracks, stems, DAW project files, some of your previous work, instruments, and any other gear unique to your sound.

What's the difference between mixing and mastering?

Mixing takes your separate recorded tracks — vocals, drums, guitars, bass — and blends them into one balanced song. Mastering is the final pass on that finished mix: the polish that makes it loud, clear, and ready to play anywhere.

What are your cancellation, revision, delivery, and payment policies?

A deposit is required to confirm your booking and is applied toward your session total. Setup and cleanup happen within your booked time. A late cancellation or no-show may forfeit your deposit, and rescheduling depends on studio and engineer availability. Extra editing, mixing, mastering, or add-on work beyond your original session is handled through a simple additional-services authorization — we'll quote the work, get your approval, and add it to your total. During your session: no smoking or alcohol, you're responsible for your guests, and we ask you to help keep the space clean; you're responsible for any damage caused during your visit.
