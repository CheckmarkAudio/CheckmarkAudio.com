# Hero Title Treatment Study

Status: Option 1 selected by Bridget and implemented as the shared root-site hero treatment on 2026-08-23. The image boards remain design references.

## Shared goals

- Remove the thin outlined rectangle and obvious glass-panel boundary.
- Keep the approved hero photography vivid outside the title area.
- Preserve the existing gold editorial type, condensed eyebrow, supporting copy, and calls to action.
- Concentrate contrast directly behind the words and feather it smoothly to clear.
- Adapt the same title system across the homepage, Services, Studio A, and the remaining inner pages.

## Option 1 — Atmospheric veil

A left-anchored charcoal gradient is darkest behind the title and fades broadly into the image. Bridget selected this direction for the active website on 2026-08-23.

## Option 2 — Soft focus pool

A smaller organic blur/shadow field hugs the copy rather than covering a fixed portion of the hero. It preserves the most photography but will need page-specific tuning when an image is especially bright or detailed behind the title.

## Option 3 — Cinematic bottom fade

The copy moves into a lower-left title lockup over a sweeping bottom-left shadow fade. This feels the most like an album or film title card and provides the biggest compositional change, but it requires the most responsive-layout testing.

## Recommended next step

Choose one overall direction, then build a browser-based prototype against the real page files. Test it at desktop, tablet, and mobile widths, with per-page gradient-position variables rather than replacing or dimming the approved photographs.
