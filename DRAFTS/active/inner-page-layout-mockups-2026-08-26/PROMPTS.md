# Production prompts

All concepts are high-fidelity 16:9 desktop website mockups for Checkmark Audio. Shared brand constraints: deep black, charcoal, warm cream, champagne gold, restrained signal-red accent, editorial high-contrast serif, expressive italic, condensed uppercase labels, authentic recording-studio photography, no invented claims, no pricing, no fabricated testimonials, no extra team members, no watermark. Shared navigation: `CHECKMARK AUDIO`, `HOME`, `SERVICES`, `STUDIO A`, `STUDIO B`, `TEAM`, `COMMUNITY`, `Q & A`, and `BOOK A FREE CONSULTATION`.

## Services

Creative brief from Bridget, 2026-08-26: the services page should make Checkmark Audio's services and skills feel effortlessly exceptional—confident enough not to over-explain. Use the monumental fashion-editorial and cinematic music-carousel references as the primary layout inspiration. Preserve the approved service names and starting-price model, but keep the first-screen mockups sparse and image-led.

### Concept 01 — monumental service drop

Black fashion-campaign layout with enormous white `SERVICES` across the top, a narrow service-name navigation row, and two dominant side-by-side authentic session photographs below. The page feels like a collection launch: `RECORD`, `PRODUCE`, `MIX`, `MASTER`, `LIVE`, `ARTIST MEDIA`. One understated line: `COME WITH AN IDEA. LEAVE WITH A RECORD.`

### Concept 02 — cinematic service carousel

Deep-black music-site frame around one full-bleed authentic session photograph. The active slide is `VOCAL RECORDING`, with a small gold `01 / 06`, a restrained `FROM $50 / HOUR` label, and service names across the top as carousel controls. The image and service title carry the page; one gold outlined `PLAN A SESSION` button.

### Concept 03 — skills index

Warm cream and black asymmetric editorial index. A huge cropped serif `WHAT WE DO` sits behind a vertical numbered service list. Hovering/selected `MIXING + MASTERING` reveals one large control-room photograph and a compact line `BALANCE. IMPACT. RELEASE-READY.` Red selection dot, gold rules, no card grid.

### Concept 04 — six cuts / one record

Black modular grid made from six photographic crops of performance, workstation, instruments, microphone, live session, and artist imagery. Each crop has only a number and service name. A narrow cream title rail reads `SIX WAYS IN. ONE FINISHED RECORD.` Strong rhythm and editorial confidence; realistically codeable with CSS Grid.

## Services — round 2: tactile studio-tech system

Creative brief from Bridget, 2026-08-26: move away from conventional editorial service layouts. Fuse a diagram-like technical language with playful Y2K computer clip art, tactile textures, and restrained humor. The page should make expertise feel intuitive and effortless—not like an explainer, manual, or information dump. The supplied orange-on-black retro-computer doodle sheet controls the visual language only; none of its wording or interface claims are copied. No Discord branding is used.

### Concept 05 — signal-chain playground

Deep black textured canvas with one confident orange-gold hand-drawn signal path: `IDEA → RECORD → PRODUCE → MIX → MASTER → RELEASE`. Oversized service nodes combine studio photographs with sparse CRT, waveform, cursor, patch-cable, star, and dial doodles. One short declaration: `YOUR SOUND, FULLY REALIZED.` It reads as a visual journey rather than a lesson.

### Concept 06 — Studio OS

A luxurious retro desktop for making records: floating black and warm-cream windows, orange line-art controls, tactile grain, and real studio photography inside selected windows. Service launchers are `RECORD`, `PRODUCE`, `MIX + MASTER`, `LIVE`, `VOICE`, and `ARTIST MEDIA`. The active window says only `OPEN A SESSION` and `READY WHEN YOU ARE.` The composition stays spacious and editorial rather than becoming novelty software UI.

### Concept 07 — audio blueprint

Warm cream drafting-paper background with charcoal ink, champagne-gold rules, orange-red annotation marks, and an exploded audio signal drawn across the page. A microphone, waveform, console fader, and master output are expressed as refined technical illustrations around photographic proof points. Monumental `SERVICES` typography and the line `FROM FIRST TAKE TO FINAL FILE.` keep the tone high-end and concise.

### Concept 08 — modular service map

Premium black modular-synth-inspired service map: six large modules connected by relaxed orange patch cables, each module containing a service name, one tactile control, and selective authentic photography. Paper scuffs, halftone, tiny doodle stickers, and red status lights add personality. The message is `PLUG IN WHERE YOU NEED US.` with one restrained consultation control.

## Services — round 3: refined media integration

Bridget selected the round-two visual language and specifically locked the following elements: `SERVICES` as the page title; the illustrated `IDEA → RECORD → PRODUCE → MIX → MASTER → RELEASE` diagram across the top; the outlined consultation button; the `RECORDING FROM $50 / HOUR` micro-label; black tactile texture; warm-cream condensed type; and orange linework. Round three removes squiggly photo cables and keeps photography calmer, more rectangular, and more consistent with the rest of the website.

### Concept 09 — single cinematic window

One dominant clean rectangular photo window beneath the signal-chain diagram, paired with the `SERVICES` title and a restrained six-item service launcher. Photography appears only once in the hero; changing the selected service would swap the image. No thumbnails and no cables around media.

### Concept 10 — editorial filmstrip

A disciplined horizontal strip of three large rectangular photographs beneath the signal-chain diagram. Orange crop marks and compact service labels connect the images to the illustration language without enclosing them in novelty software windows. No overlapping images or loose cables.

### Concept 11 — illustrated monitor reveal

One oversized hand-drawn studio monitor/CRT becomes the sole media frame, with a real session photograph displayed edge-to-edge inside its screen. The signal diagram remains separate above it, while a simple service selector sits alongside the monitor. The illustration holds the photo without extra visual clutter.

### Concept 12 — cinematic split panel

The lower hero is divided into a quiet black typographic panel and one wide rectangular cinematic photograph with only a thin orange keyline. Small service icons sit in a strict rail between the two. The diagram supplies the playful technical character; the media treatment stays closest to the approved Studio A/B pages.

## Header style check — gradient-gold consultation CTA

Preserve the selected condensed Checkmark header, navigation spacing, black tactile background, orange active state, and thin lower rule. Change only `BOOK A FREE CONSULTATION`: invert the orange outline into a restrained metallic-gold horizontal gradient, with antique-gold edges, a champagne center, near-black label and starburst, and a thin dark-gold keyline. Avoid glossy yellow, plastic, bevel, or three-dimensional effects.
