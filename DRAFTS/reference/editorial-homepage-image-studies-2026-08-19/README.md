# Checkmark Audio Editorial Homepage Image Studies

Exploratory static image mockups only. These files do not alter, replace, or control the working website.

Branch: `codex/editorial-homepage-image-studies`

## Folder map

- `references/` — the five supplied editorial inspiration screenshots plus the original Checkmark Audio concept.
- `source-media/` — copies of Checkmark-owned repository photographs used in the studies.
- `mockups/` — three generated homepage concept images.

1. `01-editorial-asymmetric-grid-reference.png` — asymmetric grid, vertical rail, magazine-like hierarchy.

3. `03-editorial-monumental-type-reference.png` — monumental masthead scale and decisive image pairing.
4. `04-music-cinematic-carousel-reference.png` — cinematic music-site framing and restrained navigation.

The external screenshots are mood and composition references only. They are not templates to copy and their brands, products, copy, and marks are excluded from the Checkmark concepts.

## Mockup directions

### 01 — Asymmetric Editorial Index

Combines an art-book navigation rail with the established Checkmark serif masthead. Uses the real studio sign and control-room photographs in a two-part editorial image field. The bottom review strip connects the experimental layout back to the original Checkmark concept.

### 02 — Warm Campaign Collage

The most disruptive direction. Uses a champagne campaign field, oversized cropped CHECKMARK typography, the real blue-lit artist/photographer portrait as the central card, and the studio sign as a smaller place marker.

### 03 — Dark Cinematic Artist Homepage

The most music-forward direction. Uses the real black-and-white artist portrait as the main cinematic field, a narrow warm studio-sign panel, concise recording-service actions, and a cream review drawer based on the original concept.

## Shared content constraints

- Checkmark Audio remains an Albuquerque recording studio, not a fashion or ecommerce brand.
- Primary actions remain consultation and work-listening paths.
- The concepts use Checkmark-owned photographs copied from `MEDIA/IMAGES/`.
- Testimonials shown in generated imagery are layout placeholders and are not approved public copy.
- Generated marks and small interface details are conceptual and must not replace approved logo assets.
- None of these concepts is approved for implementation.

## Generation method

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow as three separate `ui-mockup` studies. Each prompt used the original Checkmark concept for brand grounding, one or two supplied editorial references for composition, and Checkmark-owned repository photographs for subject matter.
