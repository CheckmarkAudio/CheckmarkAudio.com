- ~~What is the difference between mixing and mastering?~~ — answered 2026-08-10 (Bridget), reworded into original copy 2026-08-10:

  **Mixing vs. Mastering**

  Mixing takes your separate recorded tracks — vocals, drums, guitars, bass, everything — and blends them into one balanced song. Mastering is the final pass on that finished mix: the polish that makes it loud, clear, and ready to play anywhere.

  **What mixing does**
  - Balances every element — vocals, drums, guitars, bass — so nothing fights for space
  - Cleans up unwanted noise and carves out room for each instrument to breathe
  - Adds character with effects like reverb and delay for depth and emotion
  - Pulls every track together so the song feels like one cohesive record

  **What mastering does**
  - Works on the finished stereo mix, not the individual tracks
  - Brings the track up to competitive commercial loudness
  - Fine-tunes tone with high-end EQ and compression to smooth out any remaining rough edges
  - Preps and formats the file for Spotify, Apple Music, vinyl, radio, and everywhere else your music plays

  **Which do you need?**
  - **Mixing** — if your raw multi-track recording still needs balance, tuning, and creative shaping
  - **Mastering** — if your mix is already finished and just needs final loudness, polish, and distribution-ready formatting
  - **Both** — if you want us to take your raw session all the way from messy tracks to a radio-ready record
