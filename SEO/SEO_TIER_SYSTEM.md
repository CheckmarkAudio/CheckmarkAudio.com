# Seven-Tier SEO Operating System

Review every task in this order; a lower tier cannot compensate for failure above it.

1. **Technical SEO:** crawlability, indexability, canonicals, sitemap, robots, links, mobile performance, metadata, and structured data.
2. **Page intent:** one audience, primary service, search intent, and conversion goal.
3. **Local SEO:** natural Albuquerque context, accurate facts, local proof, and Google Business Profile alignment.
4. **Content quality:** audience, process, deliverables, preparation, pricing factors, proof, FAQs, and next action.
5. **Metadata and structure:** unique title and description, one H1, logical headings, clean URL, and natural terminology.
6. **Image SEO:** authentic media, correct folder, descriptive filename, dimensions, compression, contextual alt text, and permission.
7. **Conversion:** clear CTA, functional contact path, trust, pricing guidance, and reduced confusion.

Write metadata after content is stable. Optimize media after its page purpose is known. Finish by testing and measuring the action path.
