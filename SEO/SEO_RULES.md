# SEO Rules

SEO begins before a page, asset, or campaign is created. Confirm facts; define audience, intent, and conversion; apply the seven tiers in order; preserve repository rules; test mobile and desktop; and record changes and measurement.

## Non-negotiable

- One authoritative page owns each major intent.
- Never invent claims, credentials, reviews, prices, client names, policies, or permissions.
- Local relevance must be real, not repeated mechanically.
- Content and proof outrank filename or metadata tricks.
- Do not create doorway pages, duplicate keyword variants, or conflicting service promises.
- URLs are short, stable, lowercase, descriptive, and hyphenated.
- Redirect and test every replaced public URL.
- Structured data must match visible confirmed facts.
- Wix remains live until the replacement passes approval and launch QA.
- The school is a separate website and owns school search intent.

## Claim Classification

- Confirmed guidance: supported by a primary source.
- Likely indirect benefit: improves comprehension, accessibility, usability, or organization.
- Conversion practice: helps users act but is not necessarily a ranking factor.
- Internal workflow: improves consistency and safety.
- Speculation: do not present as fact.
