# Voice and Tone

Write clearly, concretely, and helpfully. Lead with the client's outcome, explain process and evidence, acknowledge boundaries, and make the next action obvious.

Prefer: professional, experienced, clear, personalized, hands-on, release-ready, polished, supportive, and transparent.

Avoid unless proven: world-class, number one, unbeatable, guaranteed, cheapest, or vague hype such as “insane quality.”

Use short paragraphs, informative headings, useful labels, and natural Albuquerque references. Preserve the client's language in reviews and never strengthen a testimonial deceptively.

## No wordy captions (Bridget, repeated standing rule)

Default to a title alone. Do not add an explanatory sentence, helper caption, or "what this is" note under UI elements (hero subtext, badges, embeds, cards, section headers) unless the concept genuinely cannot be conveyed by the title/price/tagline by itself. Never write internal-process or dev notes as if they were public copy — those belong in `START_HERE/` or `SEO/` tracking docs, not on the page. Hero subtext in particular should be a short line, not a sentence explaining what the visitor is looking at.
