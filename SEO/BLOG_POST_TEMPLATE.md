# Blog Post Template

Create supporting content only after core technical, homepage, service, contact, team, pricing, proof, and internal-link work is sound.

- Real audience question and purpose
- Named Checkmark Audio expert
- Original operational evidence or primary-source research
- One service page the article supports
- Non-competing working title and URL
- Direct answer, useful sections, examples, limitations, and next step
- Natural internal links and attribution
- Claim, permission, metadata, mobile, and conversion review

Do not publish generic AI copy merely to increase page count.
