# Asset Naming Rules

## Required Formula

`[service]-[location when useful]-[specific description]-[brand when useful].[format]`

Use lowercase kebab-case. Location and brand are conditional, not mandatory. Names must describe the actual media without workflow noise, unsupported claims, or keyword chains.

Every image visibly showing Checkmark Audio's own logo must include both `checkmark-audio` and `logo` in its filename. Describe meaningful variants factually with words such as `gold`, `white`, `black`, `transparent`, `headphones`, or `microphone`. Third-party and separate-brand logos retain their actual organization or brand names. Add `albuquerque-nm` only when it truthfully clarifies a local studio, room, exterior, service, or primary business-identity asset—not merely because an image is decorative.

Good examples from the training manual:

- `vocal-recording-albuquerque-checkmark-audio-booth.webp`
- `band-recording-studio-a-live-room.webp`
- `gavin-hammond-audio-engineering-instructor.webp`
- `studio-b-voice-over-microphone-setup.webp`

Reject:

- Camera defaults such as `IMG_4421.JPG`
- Workflow names such as `studio-final-final2.png`
- Stuffing such as `best-cheap-recording-studio-albuquerque-near-me.webp`
- Irrelevant locations such as `albuquerque-gold-arrow-icon.svg`

Keep source/web distinctions only when two variants coexist. Store catalog IDs, hashes, permissions, credits, and page assignments in the inventory rather than the public filename.
