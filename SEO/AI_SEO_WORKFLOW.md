# AI SEO Workflow

AI may research, organize, outline, compare, audit, draft, and document. It cannot independently approve business facts or public claims.

1. Request: define the business need and result.
2. Research: read repository governance, facts, current pages, and evidence.
3. Brief: define audience, intent, CTA, keywords, proof, and dependencies.
4. Draft.
5. Subject review.
6. SEO review using the seven tiers.
7. Technical QA.
8. Human approval.
9. Publish with a change record.
10. Measure business outcomes.

Final public material must be checked by someone who understands the service and can verify every factual claim.
