# Local SEO and Search Intent

## Studio-Site Priorities

- Highest: recording studio Albuquerque; vocal recording Albuquerque; mixing and mastering Albuquerque.
- High: band/drum recording Albuquerque; voice-over recording Albuquerque.
- Supporting: studio time, music production, and podcast recording Albuquerque.
- Informational: cost, preparation, process, comparison, and timeline questions.

School keywords belong to the separate school website.

Use one primary topic and two to five related supporting terms per page. Use Albuquerque only where the service is genuinely local. Never use keyword chains, repeated “near me,” or the obsolete meta-keywords tag.

Maintain one approved business name, address, phone, hours, website, and contact path across the website, Google Business Profile, directories, schema, and templates.
