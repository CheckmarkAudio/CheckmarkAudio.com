5. Add canonicals, unique social previews, truthful schema, a complete production sitemap, tested permanent redirects, conversion measurement, and approved indexing rules. Validate exact final URLs before removing noindex.
6. Deploy staging to the intended production system. Test all ten pages, performance, keyboard/focus/contrast/reduced motion, media, inquiry send/receipt/error states, every consultation entry point, timezone/conflict behavior, real 404s, HTTPS, security/privacy controls, and approved metadata.
7. Obtain Bridget's explicit launch approval. Preserve Wix and DNS backups, TLS and mail-related records; switch the web domain in a controlled window, verify production, monitor errors/conversions/indexing, and retain an executable rollback. Retire Wix hosting only after acceptance; domain registration and email must not be inadvertently canceled.

This is principally a hosting move on the same business domain, with page-path changes where approved.
