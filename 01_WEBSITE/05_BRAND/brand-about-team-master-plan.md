# About Page Requirements

The About page should explain why Checkmark exists and why it matters in Albuquerque.

## Must Include

- Mission statement.
- Albuquerque music community focus.
- Studio origin story.
- Gavin's experience.
- Why Checkmark exists.
- Professional standards.
- Community vision.
- Connection to education and artist development.

# Team Page Requirements

The team page must be current and accurate. No outdated staff should remain public.

## Each Team Member Needs

- Name.
- Role.
- Photo.
- Short bio.
- Best for.
- Services they handle.
- Starting rate, if applicable.
- Book with this person button, if applicable.
