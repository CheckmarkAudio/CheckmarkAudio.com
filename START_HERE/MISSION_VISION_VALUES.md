# Mission, Vision And Values

These statements consolidate the direction already present in the source-of-truth and planning files. Bridget and Gavin should approve or revise the wording before it becomes final public copy.

## Mission

Help artists, singers, bands, podcasters, and creators record, produce, mix, and master release-ready work in a professional Albuquerque studio where they receive practical guidance and personal attention.

## Vision

Raise the standard for music creation in New Mexico by giving local artists access to professional production, trusted collaborators, artist development, and a clear path from an idea to a finished release.

## Values

- **Artist first:** Listen to the person and the goal before choosing the process.
- **Professional standards:** Deliver careful engineering and release-ready results without lowering expectations.
- **Clear guidance:** Make services, pricing, preparation, and next steps understandable.
- **Honest collaboration:** Communicate directly, set realistic expectations, and solve problems together.
- **Local investment:** Strengthen Albuquerque and New Mexico's creative community.
- **Education and growth:** Share knowledge and help artists build skills, confidence, and independence.
- **Respect for the work:** Treat every project, client, room, and file with care.
