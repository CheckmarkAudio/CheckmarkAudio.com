# Website Goals

The website is a sales and trust system, not merely a brochure.

## A First-Time Visitor Must Understand

- What Checkmark Audio does.
- Who the studio works with.
- What services are available and what they cost or how quotes work.
- What Checkmark's work sounds like.
- What the studio, sessions, and finished work look like.
- Why the team and studio are trustworthy.
- Where the studio is and how to contact it.
- How to book, call, text, or request a free consultation without confusion.
- How to use the embedded Cal.com calendar and receive a branded Checkmark Audio booking confirmation without leaving the replacement-site experience.
- How Checkmark Audio School connects to the studio without overtaking the studio website.

## Primary Outcomes

1. Increase qualified consultation and booking inquiries.
2. Reduce uncertainty around services, pricing, studios, and process.
3. Prove quality through approved audio, video, photos, credits, and reviews.
4. Make mobile calling, texting, and booking effortless.
5. Establish strong Albuquerque identity and trustworthy local-business information.
6. Replace Wix without breaking the current public experience or domain.
7. Own the booking journey on the replacement site through Cal.com and the branded EmailJS communication flow.

## Experience Standard

- Professional and editorial rather than oversized or gimmicky.
- Real Checkmark Audio work and people rather than generic decoration.
- Alternating light and dark sections with strong readability.
- Clear navigation, focused calls to action, and no hidden essential information.
- Fast, responsive, accessible, and easy to maintain by Bridget, Gavin, or another handler.

## Measures Of Success

- Consultation and booking-form completions.
- Tap-to-call and tap-to-text actions.
- Engagement with pricing, services, studios, and proof.
- Audio/video plays and portfolio interaction.
- Local-search visibility and accurate business information.
- Fewer repetitive questions before a qualified inquiry.
