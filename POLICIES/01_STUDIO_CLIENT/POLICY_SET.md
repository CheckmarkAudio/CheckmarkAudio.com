# Studio Client Policy Set

**Approved as final: Gavin, via Bridget, 2026-08-10.** This document is the policy — no further owner sign-off needed.

This is the consolidated framework for studio-client policies. It is populated only with rules supported by the reviewed discussion or archived policy requirements. Missing business decisions are shown explicitly rather than invented.

## 1. Booking, deposit, cancellation, rescheduling, and no-shows

Confirmed direction from the existing Studio B agreement described in discussion:

- A deposit is required to confirm a rental booking.
- The deposit is applied to the session total.
- Setup and cleanup occur during booked time.
- A late cancellation or no-show may forfeit the deposit.
- Rescheduling depends on engineer and studio availability.

Still needs owner approval: deposit amount/percentage, cancellation deadline, rescheduling limit, late-arrival treatment, emergency exception, no-show definition, and whether any remainder beyond the deposit is due.

## 2. Payment, failed payment, refunds, and payment arrangements

Studio-wide terms were not finalized in the reviewed discussions.

Still needs owner approval: when balances are due, accepted methods, tax handling, failed-payment fee, late-payment fee, refund eligibility, dispute process, and who may approve written exceptions.

Do not copy the audio-school $75/$35 terms into studio-client policy without an explicit decision; those figures came from school documents.

## 3. Revisions and additional services

Confirmed direction:

- Extra editing, mixing, mastering, vocal tuning, additional filming, extra studio time, rush delivery, extra versions, and raw-file/stem delivery can be handled through one Additional Services Authorization.
- The authorization should identify the related project, selected work, flat or hourly price, quantity, total, deposit/payment due, delivery estimate, and client approval.

Still needs owner approval: revisions included per service, what counts as a revision, revision deadline, turnaround, recall fees, and rush rates.

## 4. File delivery and storage

The policy should address delivery formats, download deadlines, archive period, client backup responsibility, session/project files, stems, raw media, and charges for later retrieval or delivery.

No retention period or file-delivery charge was approved in the reviewed discussions.

## 5. Client content and rights responsibility

The policy should establish that clients are responsible for permissions relating to material they bring to the studio, including compositions, beats, samples, loops, performances, images, and third-party recordings.

Exact warranty, indemnity, ownership, and licensing language requires legal review and was not approved in the reviewed discussions.

## 6. Conduct, guests, cleanup, equipment, and damage

Confirmed direction from the existing rental agreement described in discussion:

- No smoking or alcohol.
- Renters are responsible for their guests.
- Renters are responsible for damage they cause.
- Cleaning expectations apply.

Still needs owner approval: guest limits, age/supervision rules, prohibited conduct, food/drink boundaries, damage assessment/payment process, safety stop-work authority, and termination of a session.

## 7. Approval gate

Before this becomes an operating policy, Bridget/Gavin should resolve every “still needs owner approval” item, compare the result against the actual current studio contracts, and obtain legal review for payment, refund, rights, liability, privacy, and terms language.
