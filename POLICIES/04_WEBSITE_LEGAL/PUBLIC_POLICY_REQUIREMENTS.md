# Public Website Policy Requirements

The archived website-policy plan identifies these public sections:

1. Deposit policy.
2. Cancellation policy.
3. No-show policy.
4. Refund policy.
5. Revision policy for mixes and masters.
6. File delivery policy.
7. Client responsibility policy.
8. Studio behavior policy.
9. Privacy policy.
10. Terms of service.

## Publication rule

Do not publish invented or placeholder policy language. Publish only sections expressly approved by Bridget/Gavin; hide unfinished sections. Privacy, terms, refunds, rights, and liability language require special care and legal review.

## Intended links

- Booking/inquiry flow to deposits and cancellations.
- Pricing to deposits and the approved custom-quote disclaimer.
- FAQ answers to matching cancellation and revision sections.
- Footer to privacy and terms/policies.

No root website page is created by this policy-library work.
