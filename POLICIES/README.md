# Checkmark Policy Library

This folder is the organized working library for Checkmark Audio policy documents and policy-related forms recovered from recent ChatGPT discussions and repository references.

Nothing in this folder is automatically approved, published, or legally binding. Each document states its own status. Owner approval and, where noted, New Mexico legal review are required before operational or public use.

## Start here

1. [`00_INDEX/POLICY_DOCUMENT_REGISTER.md`](00_INDEX/POLICY_DOCUMENT_REGISTER.md) — master list, source, status, and next action.
2. [`00_INDEX/CLAUDE_POLICY_HANDOFF.md`](00_INDEX/CLAUDE_POLICY_HANDOFF.md) — ready-to-copy prompt for completing policy decisions with Claude.
3. [`00_INDEX/DISCUSSION_AUDIT.md`](00_INDEX/DISCUSSION_AUDIT.md) — what was found in the accessible recent ChatGPT history.
4. [`01_STUDIO_CLIENT/POLICY_SET.md`](01_STUDIO_CLIENT/POLICY_SET.md) — consolidated studio-client policy framework.
5. [`02_CHECKMARK_TONIGHT/POLICY_AND_FORM_SET.md`](02_CHECKMARK_TONIGHT/POLICY_AND_FORM_SET.md) — sponsored appearance and optional add-on documents.
6. [`03_SCHOOL_AND_LESSONS/POLICY_AND_FORM_SET.md`](03_SCHOOL_AND_LESSONS/POLICY_AND_FORM_SET.md) — school and piano policy/form register, kept separate from the studio website.
7. [`04_WEBSITE_LEGAL/PUBLIC_POLICY_REQUIREMENTS.md`](04_WEBSITE_LEGAL/PUBLIC_POLICY_REQUIREMENTS.md) — website policy sections still awaiting approved language.

## Filing rule

- `01_STUDIO_CLIENT/`: policies for recording, rental, mixing, mastering, production, and client sessions.
- `02_CHECKMARK_TONIGHT/`: Checkmark Tonight appearance, sponsorship, release, and add-on documents.
- `03_SCHOOL_AND_LESSONS/`: audio-school and piano-teaching documents. These are not studio-website policy pages.
- `04_WEBSITE_LEGAL/`: public-facing privacy, terms, and policies-page requirements.
- `99_REFERENCE/`: source notes and historical material only.
