## C. Studio-client policy documents identified as needed

These are the policy subjects that should support the reusable contracts and the future website. They are consolidated to avoid a separate form for every edge case.

1. Booking, deposits, cancellation, rescheduling, late arrival, and no-shows.
2. Payments, failed payments, refunds, and approved payment arrangements.
3. Mix/master revision limits and additional-services authorization.
4. File delivery, download deadlines, archive/storage, and client backups.
5. Client content, permissions, samples, and rights responsibility.
6. Studio conduct, guests, smoking/alcohol rules, cleanup, equipment, and damage.
7. Media releases and promotional-content permissions.
8. Privacy policy.
9. Website terms of service.
