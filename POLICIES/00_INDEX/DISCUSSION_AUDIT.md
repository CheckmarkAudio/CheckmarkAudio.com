# Recent ChatGPT Policy Discussion Audit

## Accessible discussions reviewed

### Late Payment Policy Summary

The discussion reviewed six uploaded school documents and later created/revised a client-specific payment addendum.

Confirmed policy terms from the uploaded documents:

- A $75 late fee applies when payment is more than five calendar days late.
- A separate $35 fee applies to each returned or failed payment.
- Instruction may be paused when the account is 10 calendar days past due until the account is brought current.
- The signed Enrollment Agreement controls if it conflicts with the Student Handbook.
- The reviewed wording did not establish interest, recurring weekly/monthly late fees, automatic termination, collections or attorney fees, or a post-pause payment deadline.

The later addendum was specific to one student and one overdue Module 2 installment. It should not be converted into a school-wide rule without reviewing the signed source documents and obtaining the appropriate compliance/legal review.

### Band Service Agreement

The discussion produced and revised:

- A Checkmark Tonight Sponsored Guest Appearance Agreement.
- A separate Optional Post-Production Editing Authorization.
- A final recommendation to keep only four core contract generators plus one add-on form.

The working direction was intentionally consolidated after the user rejected a much larger list of stand-alone agreements.

Important confirmed decisions:

- The sponsored appearance document should show full service value, an equal sponsorship credit, and an unmistakable $0 balance when fully sponsored.
- Optional editing belongs in a separate authorization rather than inside the sponsored appearance agreement.
- Public reviews must remain voluntary and honest; delivery should not be conditioned on a five-star review.
- Media releases need individual performer signatures as well as the band representative's signature.
- The print version should work in black and white.
