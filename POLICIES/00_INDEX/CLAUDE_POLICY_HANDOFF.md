### Interview method

Work through one policy area at a time. Ask short, plain-language questions in manageable groups. Explain consequences or common edge cases when that helps me decide, but do not pressure me toward invented defaults.

For each answer, distinguish:

- **Confirmed decision** — I explicitly approved it.
- **Draft preference** — I expressed a direction but did not finalize it.
- **Still open** — I have not decided.
- **Legal-review item** — wording or enforceability needs professional review.
- **Service-specific** — the rule varies by recording, mixing, mastering, production, rental, photography/video, or Checkmark Tonight.

If my new answer conflicts with an existing file, show me the conflict and ask which rule should control. Do not silently reconcile it.

### Required decision checklist

#### 1. Deposits and booking confirmation

- Which services require a deposit?
- Flat amount or percentage for each service?
- When is a booking considered confirmed?
- When is the remaining balance due?
- Is the deposit refundable, transferable, or creditable?
- What happens if Checkmark Audio cancels?

#### 2. Cancellation and rescheduling

- Required notice period
- How notice must be given
- Treatment of the deposit and other payments at each notice interval
- Number of permitted reschedules
- Deadline for using a transferred credit
- Emergency exceptions and who approves them
- Studio/engineer unavailability
- Weather, illness, power, equipment, or force-majeure situations

#### 3. No-shows and late arrivals

- Definition of a no-show
- Grace period, if any
- Whether a no-show forfeits the deposit or full session price
- Whether late sessions still end at the scheduled time
- Overtime rules
- What happens if Checkmark Audio is late

#### 4. Refunds

- Refundable versus nonrefundable payments
- Refunds for unused studio time
- Refunds after work has begun
- Treatment of custom work and third-party expenses
- Method and expected processing window
- Dispute/escalation contact

#### 5. Revisions by service

For recording/editing, mixing, mastering, music production, photography/video, and any other active service:

- Number of included revision rounds
- Definition of one revision round
- Deadline for submitting consolidated notes
- What counts as a correction versus a creative change
- Price or quoting method for extra revisions
- Effect of new files, stems, performances, or changed direction
- Approval/finalization process
- Recall or reopening policy

#### 6. Delivery and storage

- Standard deliverables and formats by service
- Delivery method
- Turnaround language: fixed deadline, estimate, or project-specific quote
- When the timeline begins
- Effect of delayed client feedback or missing files
- Download-window length
- Studio archive/storage period
- Client backup responsibility
- Raw files, stems, project/session files, and alternate versions
- Retrieval, transfer, or drive costs
- Final approval and correction procedure

#### 7. Studio-client payments

- Accepted payment methods
- Deposit and balance timing
- Applicable tax treatment
- Late-payment rules and fees, if any
- Failed/returned-payment rules and fees, if any
- Whether work or delivery pauses for nonpayment
- Written payment arrangements and who may approve them
- Chargeback/dispute contact process
- Receipts and invoicing

#### 8. Privacy

- Information collected through inquiry/contact forms
- Why it is collected and how it is used
- EmailJS, Cal.com, analytics, hosting, Supabase, and other actual processors
- Cookies/analytics choices
- Retention periods
- Access and deletion request contact
- Client files and unreleased-project confidentiality
- Photography/video/media-consent separation
- Minors' information
- Security and breach-response wording requiring legal/technical review

Do not draft the final privacy policy until the website's actual data flows and vendors have been verified.

#### 9. Studio behavior and safety

- Smoking, vaping, alcohol, drugs, weapons, harassment, and illegal conduct
- Food and drink boundaries
- Maximum guests and who may enter each room
- Children/minors and supervision
- Animals/service animals
- Session conduct and staff stop-work authority
- Volume/hearing and equipment safety
- Cleanup and trash
- Renter/client responsibility for guests
- Damage reporting, assessment, and payment
- Prohibited equipment use
- Photos, filming, livestreaming, and confidentiality
- Accessibility or accommodation requests
