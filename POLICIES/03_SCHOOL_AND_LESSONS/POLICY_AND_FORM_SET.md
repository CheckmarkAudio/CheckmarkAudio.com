# School and Piano Policy and Form Set

The audio school remains on its separate website. These documents are tracked here for organizational completeness but must not be copied onto the studio website as though they were studio-client policies.

## Audio School Enrollment Contract

The practical generator described in discussion should cover student information, program dates and schedule, tuition/tax, payment plan and installment dates, late-payment terms, attendance, withdrawal/refund terms, safety acknowledgment, optional media consent, handbook acknowledgment, and signatures.

## Confirmed late-payment terms from reviewed school documents

- $75 late fee after payment is more than five calendar days past due.
- $35 per returned or failed payment.
- Instruction may be paused at 10 calendar days past due until the account is current.
- Signed Enrollment Agreement controls if it conflicts with the handbook.

These statements must be verified against the actual current signed master documents before reuse.

## Client-specific Payment-Date Addendum

The recovered discussion created an addendum for one student's overdue Module 2 balance. It preserved an existing $75 late charge, supplied a replacement payment date, and described consequences of continued nonpayment. This is a client-specific exception form, not a reusable school-wide policy.
