# Checkmark Tonight Policy and Form Set

## 1. Sponsored Guest Appearance Agreement

The recovered specification calls for one combined packet containing:

- Event and band information.
- Production duration.
- Lead engineer and assistant engineer hours/rates.
- Total staff-hours and standard production value.
- Sponsorship credit.
- Final balance due, with a prominent `$0 DUE — NO PAYMENT REQUIRED` statement when fully sponsored.
- Deliverables and delivery target.
- Production terms.
- Band representative authorization/signature.
- Individual media releases and signatures for every performer.
- Black-and-white printer-safe formatting.

Rates and hours changed several times for the specific event. They are project inputs, not permanent policy.

## 2. Additional Services Authorization

This is a separate short form, not a clause inside the sponsored agreement.

Fields:

- Related event/project.
- Authorized additional service.
- Deliverables.
- Flat fee or hourly rate.
- Quantity/hours and total.
- Included correction/revision round, if any.
- Payment due.
- Delivery estimate.
- Client approval and signatures.

The reviewed event used a $100 optional editing fee. Do not treat that as a universal price without owner approval.

## 3. Review and feedback rule

- Private feedback may be requested.
- Any public review must be voluntary and honest.
- Do not condition delivery, sponsorship, free service, or a discount on a five-star or otherwise positive review.
